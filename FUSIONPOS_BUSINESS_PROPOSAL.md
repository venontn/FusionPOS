## Company Vision

Fusion 5 Technologies will prioritize **affordability**, **reliability**, **usability**, and **scalability**. The platform will be designed to meet the operational realities of Zimbabwean businesses while remaining adaptable for future regional expansion.

The long-term vision is to improve efficiency, reduce losses, strengthen reporting, and support informed business decision making through data-driven insights.

---

## 1.0 Executive Summary

FusionPOS is a proposed offline-first smart point of sale platform developed by Fusion 5 Technologies for Zimbabwean retail businesses. The system is designed to continue operating during internet outages while automatically synchronizing records when connectivity returns.

FusionPOS combines sales processing, inventory management, reporting, cashier management, and future machine learning insights to help retailers improve decision making and operational efficiency.

---

## 2.0 Introduction and Background

Many retail businesses in Zimbabwe experience unstable internet connectivity, power interruptions, manual stock tracking, and weak reporting systems. Existing POS solutions often depend on constant internet access or are too expensive for small and medium retailers.

FusionPOS seeks to address these gaps through a practical, affordable, and intelligent retail platform tailored for local realities.

---

## 3.0 Problem Statement

Retail businesses frequently face stock losses, inaccurate manual accounting, delayed reporting, poor inventory visibility, and operational disruptions caused by unreliable internet services. These problems reduce profitability and customer trust.

A reliable POS platform capable of operating offline while providing smart analytics is needed.

---

## 4.0 Proposed Solution: FusionPOS

FusionPOS will provide a secure, scalable, and easy-to-use point of sale platform supporting sales, inventory, suppliers, reporting, and customer management.

The system will operate offline using a local database and synchronize data to the cloud automatically when connectivity is restored.

---

## 5.0 Core Features

Key features include:

- Barcode scanning
- Receipt printing
- Inventory management
- Cashier accounts
- Sales dashboards
- Supplier management
- Returns and refunds
- Low stock alerts
- Mobile money support
- AI-ready reporting modules for future forecasting

---

## 6.0 Market Opportunity in Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe has a growing retail sector consisting of supermarkets, grocery shops, pharmacies, restaurants, hardware shops, and rural wholesalers. Many still depend on manual systems or expensive imported software.

FusionPOS can provide a lower-cost and locally adapted alternative.

---

## 7.0 Business Model and Pricing

FusionPOS will use a hybrid business model. Customers may purchase a once-off installation package or subscribe to premium cloud and analytics services.

| Package | Pricing |
|---------|---------|
| Basic installation | USD 50–150 (one-time) |
| Premium cloud & analytics | USD 5–20 per month (optional) |

Pricing depends on services required.

## 8.0 Technical Architecture

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| Frontend | React + Electron |
| Offline storage | SQLite |
| API | Django REST Framework |
| Cloud storage | PostgreSQL |
| Sync | Reconciliation engine for offline transactions when internet returns |
