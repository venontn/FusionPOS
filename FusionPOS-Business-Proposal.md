## 1.0 Executive Summary

FusionPOS is an **offline-first point of sale (POS) system** designed specifically for retail businesses operating in Zimbabwe. The platform combines sales processing, inventory management, reporting, cashier management, and a roadmap toward machine-learning-driven business insights.

FusionPOS is built to prioritize **affordability, reliability, usability, and scalability**—enabling small and medium retailers to modernize operations without depending on constant connectivity or enterprise-level budgets.

This proposal outlines the market need, technical approach, business model, implementation roadmap, financial outlook, and risk mitigation strategy for bringing FusionPOS to market.

---

## 2.0 Introduction and Background

Retail businesses across Zimbabwe face persistent operational challenges:

- **Unstable internet connectivity** that disrupts cloud-only systems
- **Power interruptions** that halt transactions and data capture
- **Manual stock tracking** prone to error and theft
- **Weak or delayed reporting**, limiting informed decision-making

Many existing POS solutions are either **too expensive** for local SMEs or **too dependent on always-on internet**, making them impractical for everyday use in Zimbabwean retail environments.

FusionPOS addresses this gap with a system engineered for local realities—offline resilience, simple workflows, and pricing aligned with the market.

### Company Vision

Fusion 5 Technologies will prioritize **affordability, reliability, usability, and scalability** in every phase of FusionPOS development and deployment. Our goal is to empower Zimbabwean retailers with technology that works when and where they need it most.

## 3.0 Problem Statement

Retail operators in Zimbabwe commonly experience:

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Stock losses and shrinkage | Reduced margins and inventory inaccuracy |
| Manual accounting errors | Financial discrepancies and audit difficulty |
| Delayed reporting | Slow response to trends and stock issues |
| Poor inventory visibility | Overstocking, stockouts, and lost sales |
| Internet and power disruptions | Transaction downtime and data loss on cloud-only systems |

Without a dependable, locally appropriate POS solution, businesses struggle to compete, scale, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 4.0 Proposed Solution

FusionPOS delivers a **secure, scalable point of sale platform** with:

- **Offline-first architecture** using a local database for uninterrupted sales
- **Cloud synchronization** when connectivity is available
- Integrated modules for **sales, inventory, suppliers, reporting, and customer management**

Retailers can process transactions during outages, maintain accurate records locally, and sync to the cloud when the network allows—ensuring continuity without sacrificing central visibility.

## 7.0 Business Model

FusionPOS will use a **hybrid revenue model**:

| Tier | Description | Price Range (USD) |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **One-off installation** | Basic deployment and setup | $50 – $150 |
| **Premium subscription** | Ongoing updates, cloud sync, and support | $5 – $20 / month |

This structure lowers the barrier to entry while creating recurring revenue from businesses that require enhanced features and support.

## 8.0 Technical Architecture

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| **Frontend** | React + Electron (desktop POS client) |
| **Offline data** | SQLite (local database) |
| **Backend API** | Django REST Framework |
| **Cloud data** | PostgreSQL |
| **Sync** | Custom sync engine (offline ↔ cloud when connected) |

The architecture separates offline reliability from cloud aggregation, enabling fast local transactions and centralized reporting when online.

## 11.0 Risk Assessment

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Low market adoption** | Pilot testing with real retailers; iterative UX based on feedback |
| **Technical failures** | Robust offline design; encrypted backups; staged rollouts |
| **Security concerns** | Role-based access control (RBAC); encrypted data at rest and in transit; dedicated support channel |

Proactive pilot engagement and security-by-design reduce operational and reputational risk during launch.
